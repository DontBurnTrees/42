# ex06 - ft_print_comb2

## Subject
> Create a function that displays all different combinations of two distinct two-digit numbers (XX XX) between 00 and 99, listed in ascending order.  
> ◦ 00 01 is included, but 01 00 is not.  
> ◦ Combinations like 00 00 or 12 12 are not included because the numbers must be distinct.

## Objective

> Practice nested loops and ordering with multiple conditions.

## Going further

> Why do we add '0' to (n / 10) and (n % 10) in print_number ?  
> What happens if write is called with a pointer to an uninitialized variable ?  
> Why is n2 initialized as n1 + 1 instead of 0 ?  
> Could print_number be replaced with a single write call using a string? Why or why not ?
